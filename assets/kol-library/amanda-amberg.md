# Amanda Amberg, OD

**Current affiliation:** Partner-physician, Hollywood Eye Institute, Hollywood/Cooper City, FL (made partner 2025)

## Longer biography

Dr. Amanda Amberg is a board-certified optometric physician and partner at Hollywood Eye Institute in South Florida. A South Florida native, she earned a Bachelor of Science in Biology and a Bachelor of Vision Science from Nova Southeastern University before completing her Doctorate of Optometry at NSU's College of Optometry. She completed additional clinical training at the VA Medical Center in West Palm Beach, where she built expertise in the diagnosis and management of glaucoma, retinal disease, anterior segment pathology, keratoconus, and complex ocular surface conditions.

At Hollywood Eye Institute, Dr. Amberg specializes in comprehensive dry eye treatment, ocular surface disease management, and non-surgical aesthetic rejuvenation, working with technologies including iLux, OptiLight, radiofrequency therapy, and microneedling. She was named to Newsweek's America's Best Eye Doctors–Optometrists list in 2023 and is an active member of the American Optometric Association and the Florida Optometric Association.
