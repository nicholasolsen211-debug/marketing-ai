# Laura M. Periman, MD

**Current affiliation:** Leads Periman Eye Institute, Seattle, WA

## Longer biography

Dr. Laura M. Periman is a board-certified ophthalmologist, fellowship-trained cornea and refractive surgeon, and internationally recognized ocular surface disease expert. She leads the Periman Eye Institute in Seattle, where her practice specializes in advanced, personalized care for dry eye disease, meibomian gland dysfunction, ocular rosacea, and complex ocular surface conditions.

She completed her undergraduate studies at Willamette University in Salem, Oregon, and earned her medical degree with honors from the University of Washington School of Medicine in Seattle, where she also completed her ophthalmology residency and a fellowship in cornea and refractive surgery. Before medical school, she worked in the Molecular Biology Department at Immunex Corporation. She has authored multiple peer-reviewed publications on ocular surface disease and has served as Director of Dry Eye Services and Clinical Research in Seattle.
