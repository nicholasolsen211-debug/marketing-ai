# James G. Chelnis, MD, FACS

**Current affiliation:** Founder and Medical Director, Manhattan Face & Eye; Assistant Clinical Professor of Ophthalmology and of Medical Education, Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai; faculty, New York Eye & Ear Infirmary of Mount Sinai

## Longer biography

Dr. James G. Chelnis is a dual board-certified oculofacial plastic surgeon and ophthalmologist, and a Fellow of the American College of Surgeons. He is the founder and medical director of Manhattan Face & Eye and holds academic appointments as Assistant Clinical Professor of Ophthalmology and of Medical Education at Mount Sinai, along with a faculty role at the New York Eye & Ear Infirmary of Mount Sinai.

Dr. Chelnis earned his MD from the Jacobs School of Medicine and Biomedical Sciences at SUNY Buffalo, completed an internal medicine internship at Carney Hospital, an ophthalmology residency at the Ross Eye Institute and Women & Children's Hospital of Buffalo, and a fellowship in ophthalmic plastic and reconstructive surgery at the University of Tennessee and Vanderbilt University Medical Center. His clinical practice centers on eyelid, facial, and neck rejuvenation, with particular expertise in dry eye disease. He has presented research on five continents and serves as principal investigator on clinical trials developing new aesthetic and ocular surface treatments.
