# Jenn Chinn, OD

**Current affiliation:** Optometrist, Dr. Chinn's Vision Care, San Diego, CA (second-generation optometrist at the practice)

## Longer biography

Dr. Jenn Chinn is a glaucoma-certified optometric physician and the second-generation optometrist at Dr. Chinn's Vision Care in San Diego. She graduated from the University of Missouri–St. Louis College of Optometry in 2015 with Dean's List honors and membership in the Beta Sigma Kappa Honor Society. Her clinical training emphasized glaucoma, retinal disease, low vision, diabetes management, and corneal conditions, along with private-practice rotations in pediatric vision therapy and care for traumatic brain injury patients.

Dr. Chinn is active in organized optometry, holding memberships in the California Optometric Association, the American Optometric Association, the Optical Women's Association, and VOSH. She serves on the Board of the San Diego County Optometric Society and leads the San Diego Chapter of the Young Optometrists of America. Through VOSH mission trips, she has provided eye care in Guatemala, Mexico, and Peru, and she performs eye examinations in Spanish.
