# Michael H. Gold, MD, FAAD

**Current affiliation:** Founder and Medical Director, Gold Skin Care Center, Advanced Aesthetics Medical Spa, The Laser & Rejuvenation Center and Tennessee Clinical Research Center, Nashville, TN; Clinical Assistant Professor, Vanderbilt University School of Nursing; Adjunct Assistant Professor, Meharry Medical College School of Medicine

## Longer biography

Dr. Michael H. Gold is the founder and medical director of Gold Skin Care Center, Advanced Aesthetics Medical Spa, The Laser & Rejuvenation Center and Tennessee Clinical Research Center in Nashville, Tennessee. A board-certified dermatologist and dermatologic surgeon, he oversees the Center's operations, which have combined medical and surgical dermatology, cosmetic dermatology, aesthetic services and clinical research since 1990.

Dr. Gold is recognized worldwide for bringing leading-edge technology to dermatology and aesthetic skin care. Through his clinical research he plays an integral role in developing new pharmaceutical products and medical devices, and he presents the results regularly at national and international dermatology and cosmetic meetings. He has authored more than 400 scientific articles and 35 textbook chapters, has edited two textbooks on photodynamic therapy, and is Editor-in-Chief of the Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology and Dermatological Reviews. He helped establish SCALE (Symposium for Cosmetic Advances & Laser Education), an annual U.S. dermatologic and aesthetic meeting.
