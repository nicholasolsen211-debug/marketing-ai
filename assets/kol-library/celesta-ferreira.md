# Celesta Ferreira, OD

**Current affiliation:** Founder, Cypress Optique, Cypress, TX (founded 2021); Co-Founder, Eyesthetics

## Longer biography

Dr. Celesta Ferreira is a therapeutic optometrist, optometric glaucoma specialist, and licensed dry eye specialist, and the founder of Cypress Optique in Cypress, Texas. She earned a Bachelor of Science in Nutrition from the University of Texas at Austin (Dean's List) before completing her Doctorate of Optometry at the University of Houston College of Optometry, where she received letters of clinical excellence in family practice, pediatrics, and contact lens care.

A dry eye sufferer herself, Dr. Ferreira built her practice around comprehensive dry eye diagnosis and management using advanced in-office technology, alongside broader ocular disease treatment including cataracts, glaucoma, and diabetic retinopathy. She also co-founded Eyesthetics, extending her practice into facial aesthetics. Her recognitions include CyFair Small Business of the Year (2022) and the presidency of the Harris County Optometric Society (2024); she is also known within the profession as a dry eye expert who mentors other doctors.
