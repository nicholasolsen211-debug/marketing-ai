# Maria Sampalis, OD

**Current affiliation:** Owner, Sampalis Eye Care, Cranston, RI

## Longer biography

Dr. Maria Sampalis leads Sampalis Eye Care in Cranston, Rhode Island, where her practice treats conditions including diabetic retinopathy, hypertension, and glaucoma using modern diagnostic technology. She graduated from the New England College of Optometry in 2007 and is trained in contact lens fitting across a range of prescriptions, including multifocals.

Beyond her own practice, Dr. Sampalis is a national speaker and a widely recognized voice on corporate optometry and practice growth, writing for outlets including Optometry Times, InVision Magazine, and Corporate Optometry. Her honors include the 2016 Rhode Island Young Optometrist of the Year, 2019 Most Influential Woman in Optical, 2020 World Council of Optometry Eye Care Hero, and 2024 Women In Optometry Industry Influencer.
