# Sahil A. Dosaj, OD

**Current affiliation:** Owner, Miller Optometry, Yucaipa, CA (joined 2014; purchased the practice from Dr. Miller in 2021)

## Longer biography

Dr. Sahil A. Dosaj is the owner of Miller Optometry in Yucaipa, California. He graduated with top honors from the Western University of Health Sciences College of Optometry in Pomona, CA, in 2013, and completed a residency in ocular disease at the Indian Health Services hospital in Crownpoint, New Mexico. He joined Miller Optometry in 2014 and purchased the practice from its founder, Dr. Miller, in 2021.

Dr. Dosaj is board certified in glaucoma and ocular disease management and is trained in fitting scleral and specialty contact lenses. His current clinical focus spans primary eye care, ocular disease management, specialty contact lenses, and pre- and post-operative co-management for LASIK and cataract surgery.
