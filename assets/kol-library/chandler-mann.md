# Chandler R. Mann II, OD

**Current affiliation:** Owner, Stone Oak Vision Source, San Antonio, TX (established December 2022)

## Longer biography

Dr. Chandler R. Mann II is the owner of Stone Oak Vision Source in San Antonio, Texas. Originally from San Antonio and raised in Houston, he earned a Bachelor of Science in Human Biology from the University of Texas at Austin and his Doctor of Optometry degree from the University of Houston College of Optometry. Before opening his own practice in December 2022, he gained experience in surgical co-management, ocular disease treatment, and specialty contact lens care at Berkeley Eye Center in Houston.

Dr. Mann's practice focuses on myopia management, dry eye treatment, and aesthetic therapies across all age groups. He was named an inaugural member of The Myopia Collective and received a 2024 CooperVision Best Practices honoree award for his contributions to advancing patient care. He is married to LeAnna and has two children, and he teaches on optometry topics nationally.
