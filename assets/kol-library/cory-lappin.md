# Cory J. Lappin, OD, MS, FAAO

**Current affiliation:** Founder, The Dry Eye Center of Ohio, LLC, Cincinnati (Montgomery), OH

## Longer biography

Dr. Cory J. Lappin is the founder of The Dry Eye Center of Ohio, a practice focused exclusively on the treatment of dry eye and ocular surface disease. He earned a Bachelor of Science in Zoology from Miami University (Phi Beta Kappa, with Honors and Distinction), then completed his Doctor of Optometry and a concurrent Master of Science in Vision Science at The Ohio State University College of Optometry, followed by a residency in ocular disease at the Cincinnati Eye Institute.

Before founding his own clinic in Cincinnati, Dr. Lappin practiced at Phoenix Eye Care and the Dry Eye Center of Arizona. He is a Fellow of the American Academy of Optometry, serves as Chief Medical Editor for the Optometric Physician e-journal, and chairs Eyes On Eyecare's Corneal Health Summit. He has authored numerous articles and lectures nationally and internationally, with appearances on podcasts, radio, and TV. His honors include the 2023 Young Optometrist of the Year (Arizona Optometric Association), the American Academy of Optometry Foundation Practice Excellence Award, and the Ohio State University Alumni Association Early Professional Achievement Award.
