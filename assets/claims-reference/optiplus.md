# OptiPLUS claims reference

Source materials reviewed for the 2026-10-08 pass (PB-numbered Lumenis documents on file):

- **Patient flyer** — "OptiPLUS B2C flyer" (PB-00077490, Rev B, copyright 2025). Print layout 2 pages, web layout 6 pages; a Spanish edition has the same footer and the same seven references. It is the only controlled OptiPLUS document on file that carries a numbered reference list.
- **Informed consent form** (PB-00070410, Rev A, three differing files) and **treatment plan flyers** (PB-00070390 and PB-00070400, Rev A) and **body circle flyer** (PB-00070500, Rev A, 2023): reviewed; they contain no claims with a reference.

Materials named in earlier passes and **not on file in 2026-10-08**: the "OptiPLUS-MD Playbook" (PB-00040710, Rev C) and the "OptiLight B2C Brochure US V07" (PB-00032060US, Rev C). Rows that cite them keep their earlier "Appears in" and are marked as not re-verified.

The OptiPLUS user manuals on file (MN0203-US-Opti.R4, MN0203-CAN-Opti.R1, MN0203-EN.R00) have no PB number, so they are not named in "Appears in". They were used only to check procedural statements. The published study (Chelnis, Garcia and Hamza, 2023) was read in full from three copies on file and used to check the figures below. Indications, contraindications and warnings are on the [regulatory page](../regulatory/optiplus.md); check wording there first.

## Notes and flags

- **Rows kept, none deleted.** OP-1 to OP-3 keep their main wording and status. The unmerged branch used the same IDs OP-1 to OP-3 for different claims; those claims are described in the Qualifications of OP-1 to OP-3 and tracked as OP-7, OP-9 and OP-10. OP-4 to OP-6 come from the branch with their IDs unchanged. No row is marked superseded, because no newer controlled document on file changes or drops a row's claim; the only OptiPLUS controlled document with references on file is the flyer, and it adds rows.
- **Status changes.** The branch marked OP-4, OP-5 and OP-6 `confirmed`. They are not confirmed on main, so they are `needs review` here until someone with the playbook confirms them.
- **Playbook and brochure not on file.** The playbook (PB-00040710 Rev C) and the OptiLIGHT brochure (PB-00032060US Rev C) cited by the branch are not among the files provided on 2026-10-08. It cannot be said whether newer revisions exist. Rows citing them were not re-verified against those documents.
- **Non-PB documents omitted from "Appears in".** The branch cited a business-plan deck (slides 28-30 and 35). It has no PB number, so it is not named, as the library rules require.
- **Claims left out for lack of a reference.** The flyer's "pain relief for sensitive eyes", "no-downtime", "maintenance every 6-12 months", "redness usually disappears within a few hours" and "FDA-cleared" have no reference on the flyer. They are not claims-table rows. "FDA-cleared" is on the [regulatory page](../regulatory/optiplus.md).
- **Flyer reference numbering.** The flyer lists seven references. Reference 5 (Malanga GA, Yan N, Stark J. Postgrad Med. 2015;127(1):57-65, PMID 25526231) has no matching superscript in the extracted text of either layout. The flyer's placement of references 1, 2 and 4 against three different benefits is inferred from order, not stated.
- **Study device name.** The Chelnis paper says "NueraTight" (Bios) and never "OptiPLUS". Until the regulatory owner confirms they are the same device for labeling purposes, cite OP-2, OP-3 and OP-8 as studying "multi-frequency RF combined with OptiLIGHT IPL and gland expression", not as an OptiPLUS result.
- **Market.** The flyer is a US piece (footer "-US"). The US manual R4 has no dry eye indication; the Canadian manual does. Do not use OP-2, OP-3, OP-5 or OP-8 for dry eye in US copy until the regulatory owner settles this.
- **Out of scope by design.** No business, ROI, pricing or reimbursement figures were added, and none from quotes or commercial paperwork.
