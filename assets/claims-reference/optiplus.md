# OptiPLUS claims reference

Source materials reviewed for the 2026-10-08 pass (PB-numbered Lumenis documents on file):

- **Patient flyer** — "OptiPLUS B2C flyer" (PB-00077490, Rev B, copyright 2025). Print layout 2 pages, web layout 6 pages; a Spanish edition has the same footer and the same seven references. It is the only controlled OptiPLUS document on file that carries a numbered reference list.
- **Informed consent form** (PB-00070410, Rev A, three differing files) and **treatment plan flyers** (PB-00070390 and PB-00070400, Rev A) and **body circle flyer** (PB-00070500, Rev A, 2023): reviewed; they contain no claims with a reference.

Materials named in earlier passes and **not on file in 2026-10-08**: the "OptiPLUS-MD Playbook" (PB-00040710, Rev C) and the "OptiLight B2C Brochure US V07" (PB-00032060US, Rev C). Rows that cite them keep their earlier "Appears in" and are marked as not re-verified.

The OptiPLUS user manuals on file (MN0203-US-Opti.R4, MN0203-CAN-Opti.R1, MN0203-EN.R00) have no PB number, so they are not named in "Appears in". They were used only to check procedural statements. The published study (Chelnis, Garcia and Hamza, 2023) was read in full from three copies on file and used to check the figures below. Indications, contraindications and warnings are on the [regulatory page](../regulatory/optiplus.md); check wording there first.

## Notes and flags

- **Publication check 2026-10-10:** 12 rows. Confirmed: 2 (OP-1 and OP-2, confirmed before 2026-10-09 and not changed). Still needs review: 10 (OP-3 to OP-12). None of the 10 is confirmed: each either lacks the figure, differs in population or device, or has no cited publication.
  - **Claims the publication does not support as worded:**
    - OP-3: "over 150%" expressible glands and "over 70%" meibum clarity. Chelnis 2023 gives +107.8% (lower lid) and +192.1% (upper lid) in Table 2, and clear liquid meibum at follow-up of 54.8% (lower lid) and 64.5% (upper lid). No average or 70% figure is published.
    - OP-4: 49% increase in dermal thickness is in the abstract, but for one abdominal patient on a TriPollar device, not periorbital skin or OptiPLUS.
    - OP-5: RF time "about six minutes per side". The study used 2.5 + 2.5 min, about 5 min per side.
    - OP-7: "tightens skin ... eyelids looking noticeably revitalized". No cited paper reports this; Javate reports wrinkle reduction with a different 4-MHz RF wand.
    - OP-8: "improved meibomian gland functionality" is shown only for the combined IPL + RF + gland expression protocol (uncontrolled), not for OptiPLUS alone.
    - OP-9: "enhanced blood circulation". Rabkin and Hunt show local heat raises wound-site perfusion about threefold; no RF or OptiPLUS study.
    - OP-10: "increased collagen production". Javate reports fibril changes in two women, not increased production.
    - OP-11: 79% matches the 2023 ASDS consumer survey, but respondents were consumers (n = 3,503), not patients.
    - OP-12: "all skin types" and "safe, comfortable". Green 2011 tested a different device (Thermage CPT) on the cheek, jawline and neck in 20 patients; Chelnis excluded Fitzpatrick V-VI.
  - **Could not be opened:**
    - OP-4: full text (no open-access copy; abstract read).
    - OP-6: no publication cited; nothing to open.
    - OP-7: Javate full text (abstract read only).
