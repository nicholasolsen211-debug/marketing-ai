# UltraPulse claims reference

> **Read the regulatory page first.** Check [regulatory language](../regulatory/ultrapulse.md) before reusing any row. The Alpha intended use is general ("surgical or aesthetic applications requiring the ablation, vaporization, excision, incision or coagulation of soft tissue"), and the US and EU manuals are separate market versions. A study result in this file is a published study of fractional CO2 laser treatment. It is not a cleared indication, and in most rows the study did not use the Alpha (see each row's qualifications).

Source documents used for the "Appears in" column (control IDs and revisions confirmed from each document's own footer text):

- **PB-00054500 Rev A**, "ULTRApulse Alpha - Clinical Compendium" (Publication Compendium), 15 PDF pages (printed as two-page spreads), copyright 2023 per the library index. This is the only current PB-numbered UltraPulse piece on file that prints both the study summaries and their journal citations. Page numbers below are PDF pages.
- **PB-00058610 Rev A**, "ULTRApulse Alpha Pitch Deck", 20 pages, 2023. Quotes the conclusions of Seago 2020 and Miletta 2019 on p.15 with author and year only (no journal, volume or pages).
- Checked, with no usable referenced claim: ULTRApulse Alpha Brochure PB-00054170 Rev C (2023, A4 files are in the Eye Care library, Letter files in Aesthetics; no references printed); UPA B2B Vision Brochure PB-00054171 Rev A (2026; see Notes); UPA B2B Scars One Pager PB-00070250 Rev A (2023, no references); Alpha Cheat Sheet PB-00063290 Rev A; B2C trifolds PB-00054190, PB-00054200, PB-00054210 Rev A (no references); case studies PB-00057190, PB-00057200, PB-00063240 Rev A (testimonial).
- Not used: PB-00054170 Rev A (superseded by Rev C), PB-2000688 Rev A (superseded by Rev B; Rev B is a testimonial piece with no references and no claim row), and the 2017-era DeepFX/ActiveFX and SCAAR FX pieces (older-model marketing, as in the regulatory page).

No rows for this product existed on main or in the unmerged branch, so IDs start at UP-1. Existing rows kept: 0. Marked superseded: 0. New rows: 18 (15 Aesthetics, 3 Eye Care). Every row is `needs review`: no DOI or PMID is printed in any source, no cited paper was opened or located in this pass, and the figures below are the compendium's own abstract summaries.

## Notes and flags

- **What this file is.** The 18 rows are study results as summarized in the Alpha Clinical Compendium (PB-00054500 Rev A). A compendium listing is Lumenis choosing to circulate the paper. It is not evidence that the study used the Alpha, that the claim is cleared, or that the abstract summary matches the paper. Most summaries do not name a device; where they do, the device is the UltraPulse Encore or DeepFX/ActiveFX modes (older model), as noted in each row.
- **Year and citation mismatches inside the compendium.** The topic-year headers do not always match the journal citation (Hivnor: header 2019, citation 2014; Seago, Zhang, Waibel: header 2019, citation 2020; Miletta: header 2019, citation 2021). Where volume and pages are not printed, the row says so. No DOI or PMID is printed anywhere in these documents.
- **Studies in the compendium deliberately not turned into rows.** (a) Oncology and drug-combination results: reflectance-confocal-microscopy-guided CO2 ablation of low-risk basal cell carcinoma (Navarrete-Dechent, J Am Acad Dermatol 2019;81(4):984-988), laser-assisted photodynamic therapy of actinic keratoses (Togsverd-Bo, Br J Dermatol 166(6):1262-1269), the mouse squamous cell carcinoma model (Olesen, Lasers Surg Med), and laser-assisted drug delivery (Anderson, Adv Drug Deliv Rev 2020;153:169-184). These describe uses outside the Alpha's statements on the regulatory page and need regulatory review before any copy. (b) Topical botulinum toxin A after laser for crow's feet (Mahmoud, Dermatol Surg 41 Suppl:75-81), a periorbital study of a drug, not the laser. (c) A named-competitor comparison, ActiveFX versus DEKA SmartXide (Treacy, Prime 3(7):46-60); a comparative claim needs separate substantiation. (d) Two- and five-patient case reports, a diet comparison, a focal-plane bench study and the Gold 2012 five-patient skin-of-color report, which are not product claims.
- **Pitch deck quote with no citation.** PB-00058610 Rev A p.15 quotes "Himza, et al., 2021" ("ablative lasers are a superior, safe, and effective modality to treat damaged skin"). The deck gives no journal, and the compendium does not contain the paper, so there is no traceable reference and no row was added.
- **Eye Care pieces without references.** The Alpha brochure PB-00054170 Rev C (A4, Eye Care library) and the B2C blepharoplasty trifold PB-00054210 Rev A print no references. The Vision brochure PB-00054171 Rev A (2026) prints a reference list (items 1 to 4: a market report, two Springer articles, one Karger article, *Dermatology* 2023;222(1):74-80) but **no claim in the text carries a footnote marker except the market-size figure (item 1), which is out of scope**. References 2 to 4 are not tied to any statement, so no row was added. The statement "Blepharoplasty remains the most performed facial surgery worldwide" has no marker; do not attribute it to those references without confirmation.
- **A second Eye Care compendium exists but cannot be a source here.** The "CO2 Vision Clinical Compendium" (4 files, 2026) prints "1020736 Rev A" and no PB number, so it cannot appear in an "Appears in" cell under the library rules. Its eye-care study summaries were not turned into rows. If it is given a PB number, its Eye Care studies would be the better home for the UP-16 to UP-18 style rows.
- **Older-model material.** The SCAAR FX Benefits Poster PB-1140900 Rev B cites Husain et al., *Am J Cosmetic Surgery* 28(2), 2011 for a depth-of-ablation comparison against other CO2 devices ("up to 3.5 mm"), and the Alpha brochure and one-pager claim "up to 4 mm" in a single pulse with no citation. The two depth figures are different and neither is reused here. No Alpha engineering or competitor-comparison claims were added.
- **Business figures excluded.** Market-size, revenue and reimbursement statements in the brochures, deck, Vision brochure and PB-00078xxx letters are out of scope for this library.
- **Not opened.** No cited paper was located or read. The only thing verified in this pass is that each figure and quotation above matches the compendium (or deck) text as extracted.
