# Digital Duet claims reference

Digital Duet is an Eye Care product. Its predecessors Selecta Duet and Selecta Trio are legacy devices; none of their claims are listed here and none may be reused for Digital Duet. Check wording in [regulatory/digital-duet.md](../regulatory/digital-duet.md) first.

Source materials reviewed for this pass (newest revision on file of each):

- **Patient brochure** — "Digital Duet SLT Patient Brochure" (PB-00076030, Rev B), 2-page print, copyright 2026. Page numbers below follow the 2-page print files: p.1 is the FAQ, risks and references panel, p.2 is the inside text. Rev A (2024) is superseded by Rev B and is not used as current.
- **Presentation** — "Digital Duet 2.0 Product Presentation" (PB-00059371, Rev A), 23 slides, copyright 2026.
- **Brochure** — "Digital Duet and Trio Brochure" (PB-00022530, Rev D), 6 pages, copyright 2025. A copyright-2026 version of the same PB number and revision letter also exists (same claim text); see the Notes section.
- **Checked against, but not cited in "Appears in":** the two LiGHT trial papers kept in the product's Clinical Evidence folder, namely Gazzard G, et al. Lancet 2019;393(10180):1505–1516 (DOI 10.1016/S0140-6736(18)32213-X, read from the paper's first page) and Gazzard G, et al. Ophthalmology 2023;130(2):139–151 (DOI 10.1016/j.ophtha.2022.09.009, read from the paper's first page). They are the full texts of two sources the materials cite. No other cited paper was on file.

## Notes and flags

- **Citation mismatch for the six-year LiGHT paper (DD-4, DD-6, DD-7, DD-9).** Slide 6 of PB-00059371 Rev A and ref 16 of PB-00076030 Rev B print "Ophthalmology. 2022;129(4):427–436. DOI: 10.1016/j.ophtha.2021.11.022" with the six-year title. Ref 14 of the same Rev B brochure and the paper on file give Ophthalmology 2023;130(2):139–151 (DOI 10.1016/j.ophtha.2022.09.009). The DOI printed on the slide and in ref 16 was not checked against any registry; the title match with a 2023 volume suggests it is wrong.
- **LiGHT figures are UK trial figures.** All LiGHT results (74.2% drop-free at 36 months, 69.8% at 72 months, cost results) are from treatment-naive patients in UK hospitals, with UK cost data. Do not state them as US costs or as results for the Digital Duet device specifically; the paper text on file does not name the laser used.
- **What was verified.** For DD-4 through DD-9 and DD-15 the figures were compared with the full text of the two LiGHT papers kept in the product's Clinical Evidence folder. For all other rows only the citation as printed in the Lumenis piece was read; the cited papers were not available, so none was checked.
- **Superseded wording not carried forward (PB-00076030 Rev A, 2024).** Rev A said Digital Duet SLT "is a cost-effective therapy that has been clinically proven to provide long-term control of eye pressure" (refs 8, 10, 13), "cuts down on therapy time and reduces discomfort", and "SLT has been FDA-cleared for more than 15 years". Rev B replaces these. No Rev A row is listed because the claims no longer appear in the newest revision as written.
- **Excluded, no usable reference.** Presentation slide 4 (glaucoma prevalence, projections, blindness and undiagnosed percentages) cites four bare web addresses (glaucomapatients.org, who.int, reviewofoptometry.com, opticalimages.com) with no title, date or journal citation, so it is not listed. Slide 5 (glaucoma market shares and growth rates), slide 7 (specialist survey) and the statements "from the inventors of SLT" and "first and only digital SLT+YAG device" have no reference. "SLT has been FDA-cleared for more than 20 years" and "typically covered by Medicare" have no reference and are regulatory or reimbursement statements.
- **Excluded by scope.** Reimbursement, revenue, ROI, trade-in and CPT figures (DOC-0016923 Rev B, PB-00094731 Rev A, "Digital Duet ROI", "Laser Trade In Program 2026", presentation slide 22) are business figures and are not listed. The patient brochure's "$600 to $2500 a year" drop spending (ref 12, Lee PP, et al., Arch Ophthalmol 2006;124(1):12-19) is a pricing figure and is not listed.
- **Referenced statements in the manuals, not listed.** The Digital Duet manual UM-0001300 Rev F (pp.128–135) carries several referenced clinical statements: at 36 months in the LiGHT study, 95% of eyes treated with SLT first line were at target IOP (Gazzard 2019, Health Technol Assess 23(31):1-102); IOP reduction of 22 to 29% at 6 months and 23 to 32% at 60 months in a meta-analysis of 8 randomized trials (Leahy & White 2015, Clin Ophthalmol 9:833-841); effect durations of 22.2, 33.8 and 28.9 months for first, second and third SLT in a 52-patient study (Wang 2021, Ophthalmol Glaucoma 4(5):482-489); and posterior capsule opacification in 50% of patients within 5 years (Raza 2007, J Rawalpindi Med Coll 11(1):27-29). They are not listed because the manual is a UM-numbered document and "Appears in" is limited to PB-numbered documents. If the library allows manual numbers as sources, these are candidates for review. The 95% figure should be checked first: the Lancet 2019 paper on file reports 78.2% of SLT-treated eyes at target IOP without medication at 36 months.
- **Documents without a PB number, not used as "Appears in".** The 2026 clinical publications book, the 2023 Digital Duet and Trio clinical book, the 2026 "Digital Duet and Trio Brochure" files whose text shows PB-00022530 Rev D but with copyright 2026 and no label revision, and the Digital Duet 2.0 quick guide flyers. The 2026 brochure files carry the same claim text as DD-18.
- **Legacy products.** Claims in the Selecta Duet and Trio brochure PB-2005807 Rev E (2020), such as an "over 80% success rate for reducing IOP" and "hundreds of studies", belong to the legacy product and were not borrowed.
