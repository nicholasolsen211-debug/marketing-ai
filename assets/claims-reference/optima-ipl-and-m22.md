# Optima IPL and M22 claims reference (legacy platform)

> **LEGACY PLATFORM.** Optima IPL / M22 (eye care) and Stellar M22 (aesthetics) is a legacy platform. Optima IPL is the predecessor of OptiLIGHT, and **OptiLIGHT is the current dry eye product**. **Nothing in this file may be used for OptiLIGHT claims**, and OptiLIGHT claims may not be used here. The same statistic can appear in both files with a different source document and a different qualification; do not move a row, a figure or a citation from one product to the other. Check [regulatory language](../regulatory/optima-ipl-and-m22.md) first: the dry eye indication for this platform has not been cleared or authorized by the FDA in the United States.

## Notes and flags

- **Publication check 2026-10-10:** 14 rows. Confirmed 2 (OI-7, from the abstract only; OI-11, from full text). Still needs review 12: not supported as worded 12 (OI-1 to OI-6, OI-8, OI-9, OI-10, OI-12, OI-13, SM-1); could not be opened or located 7 (see below); no source cited 0.

**Claims the publication does not support as worded:**

- OI-1: "over 80% of rosacea patients" with MGD; the review says MGD in more than 80% of rosacea patients "in most case control series" (Table 4: 76% to 85%).
- OI-2: MGD the leading cause in "more than 85%" of patients; Lemp 2012 reports 86% of 159 qualified DED patients showing signs of MGD.
- OI-3: "86% of Dry Eye cases are caused by MGD"; Lemp 2012 reports 86% showing signs of MGD, not causation.
- OI-4: ophthalmic procedures trigger DED or aggravate existing DED; De Paiva 2006 shows LASIK-related dry eye in patients without prior dry eye (47-53% at 1 week, 12.5-35% at 6 months), not aggravation.
- OI-5: pre-op DED gives less favorable outcomes after ophthalmic procedures; Shtein 2011 (LASIK only) says refractive outcomes still tend to be good.
- OI-6: TBUT +91.3% and symptom decrease 54.7%; Dell 2017 reports TBUT +93% (site 1) and +425% (site 2), SPEED -55%. Neither 91.3% nor 54.7% is in the paper.
- OI-8: IPL "inhibits the progression of the inflammation"; neither Liu 2017 nor Yin 2018 measures progression.
- OI-9: "destroys" vessels "by thrombosis"; Kassir 2011 and Papageorgiou 2008 are skin studies of redness and telangiectasia, with no mechanism.
- OI-10: Optima IPL reduces Demodex mites; Prieto 2002 is a skin histology observation in five women, and no Optima IPL eyelid study was found.
