# AcuPulse claims reference

Check [regulatory language](../regulatory/acupulse.md) first. A reference existing for a statement does not mean the statement is an approved claim for the product. In particular, the FemTouch / vaginal atrophy rows (AP-17, AP-18) describe an application that Lumenis's own 2025 brochure and pitch deck say is not cleared by the FDA in the US.

Source materials reviewed for this pass (control IDs and revisions confirmed from each document's own footer or title text):

- **Case studies, Indian skin** - "Use of CO2 laser for the treatment of acne and burn scars in Indian skin - case studies" (Dr. Aditya Shah), PB-2010525 Rev A, 8 pages, no copyright year printed. Four files on file (A4 and Letter, press and web) carry the same revision and text. Source of AP-1 to AP-11.
- **Gold white paper** - "AcuPulse with Combo Treatment Modality: Clinical Study of Patients with Skin of Color" (Michael H. Gold, MD), January 2012, copyright 2012. The index and the front matter give PB-1106140 Rev A; the page footer prints "PB-1116140 rev A". Source of AP-12 and AP-13.
- **Skinbooster white paper** - "Clinical experience with SkinBoosters and ablative and non-ablative laser combination treatments" (De Angelis, Tretti Clementoni), PB-1007586 Rev B, 4 pages, a print date of 08.06.16 on the first page, no copyright year printed. Source of AP-14 to AP-16.
- **AcuPulse W Clinical Treatment Guide, USA only** - PB-00021600 Rev B, 8 pages, copyright 2020. Source of AP-17. (A different document, the AcuPulse W brochure for the USA, is also numbered PB-00021600, Rev A; see the regulatory page.)
- **AcuPulse and FemTouch Pitch Deck** - printed "PB-0009551_A" (seven digits, no year printed), 50 slides. Source of AP-18.

No claims file for this product existed on main or in the unmerged branch, so IDs start at AP-1. Existing rows kept: 0. Marked superseded: 0. New rows: 18.
