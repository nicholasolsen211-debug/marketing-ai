# triLIFT for Eye Care claims reference

Check wording against the [regulatory page](../regulatory/trilift.md) first (Eye Care section). A claim can have a citation and still be outside the quoted indication. Aesthetics claims for triLIFT are in a separate file, `trilift-aesthetics.md`. Claims are not borrowed between the two divisions.

Source materials reviewed for the 2026-10-08 update:

- **Patient brochure** — "triLIFT 2.0 for Eye Care - Patient Brochure" (PB-00093457, Rev A, 2026), 2 pages. This is the newest Eye Care patient piece on file. Page numbers below follow the order of the extracted text: page 1 holds the results photos, indication and references; page 2 holds the claim panels.
- **One-pager** — "triLIFT one-pager, Fight the sag, lose the bags" (PB-00094660, Rev A, no copyright year printed), 2 pages.
- **Practice brochure** — "triLIFT for Eye Care - Practice Brochure" (PB-00093456, Rev A), 2 pages. **Not on file for this update.** It is the "Appears in" source of the original rows TL-4 to TL-8. Those rows could not be re-checked against it, and nothing says it is still the newest revision.

Sources of the original rows (PB-00093457 and PB-00093456) are kept as written. Where PB-00093457 Rev A (2026) now words a claim differently, the row says so under "Qualifications" and is marked superseded for the old wording.

## Notes and flags

- **Publication check 2026-10-10:** 12 rows, 6 confirmed (TL-5, TL-6, TL-8, TL-10, TL-11, TL-12) and 6 still needs review (TL-1, TL-2, TL-3, TL-4, TL-7, TL-9).

**Claims the publication does not support as worded**

- TL-2: the brochure's 2.2-fold risk of developing eyelid-related issues is not in Doan et al. 2020; the published figure is 2.23-fold risk of severe ocular surface symptoms in a veteran population (Ansari et al., Cornea 2015).
- TL-3: the claim says 3-5% per decade; Volpi et al. 2004 says 3-8% per decade for skeletal muscle in general, not the face.
- TL-9: the protocol's 4-6 sessions, 20-30 minutes and "all skin types" have no source; the related study used four weekly sessions of about 10 minutes in total and enrolled no "all skin types" population.
- TL-4 (device caveat, not a failure): the 30% wrinkle-appearance figure is in Potekaev and Zhukova 2013 for the Maximus system only, not triLIFT.

**Could not be opened**

- TL-1: both IOVS meeting abstracts (2017;58:2696 and 2016;57(12):6189); no Europe PMC record; only secondary reports.
- TL-7: Boisnic and Branchet 2014 full text (publisher blocked, no PMC copy); the abstract has no threefold figure.
- TL-9: no source is cited for the protocol, so there was nothing to open.


- **Product-evidence naming:** TL-8 and TL-10 to TL-12 use a real published DMSt study, but that does not by itself settle whether the exact study and indication can be promoted under the triLIFT for Eye Care name in every market. The one-pager and brochure carry the study reference, not a statement that the study used triLIFT.
- **Anatomy and protocol transfer:** TL-4 to TL-7 rely on studies with other devices, treatment schedules or anatomy. The values may be useful background evidence, but should never be presented as if measured in the brochure's exact treatment.
- **Shared unresolved statistics:** TL-2 and TL-3 repeat citation problems already visible in OptiLIFT materials; repeating a number across brands does not independently corroborate it.
- **Rows whose source is not on file:** TL-4 to TL-8 were attributed to the practice brochure PB-00093456 Rev A. That file is not among the documents on file. Their status is `needs review` until someone confirms they still appear in the newest practice piece.
- **Status changes from the unmerged branch:** TL-4, TL-5, TL-6 and TL-9 were `confirmed` on the unmerged branch only. They are not confirmed on main, so the status is `needs review` here. Restore `confirmed` only after review on main.
- **Excluded:** the one-pager's eyelid-surgery market, ranking and market-growth figures (business and market-sizing content, no peer-reviewed reference) and its statement that eyelid surgery is a top-5 cosmetic procedure.
