## Notes and flags

- **Statements reviewed and left out because they have no reference on file:** "Accurate, reliable, and low intra-rater variability," "Fast examination process and results," and "Offers patient comfort as well as physician ease of use" (brochure PB-2008216 Rev. B, p.1); "Antares ... gives access to a unique feature called auto-fit, which ensures optimum accuracy in the selection of contact lenses" (p.2). These are marketing statements, not referenced claims.
- **Not claims for this library:** "Highly competitive, with great ROI" (brochure p.1) is a business claim and is out of scope.
- **Specifications are not claims.** Technical specifications (24 Placido rings, 6144 measured points, 74 mm working distance) are product facts. They differ between the brochure and the 2015 manual in places; see the [Antares regulatory page](../regulatory/antares.md), Open questions 2. The manual's statement "Class A as per ISO19980:2005 (E)" for accuracy and repeatability error is a standards statement, not a published study, and has no traceable publication on file.
- **Never borrow claims from other products.** Dry eye prevalence and MGD statistics that appear in OptiLIGHT or triLIFT material describe the condition and are not Antares claims. Add them here only if an Antares PB-numbered document cites them.
- **To add rows later,** a PB-numbered Antares document with a reference list is needed (for example a clinical brochure, a user manual with a PB number, or a deck).
