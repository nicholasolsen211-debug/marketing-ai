# triLIFT for Aesthetics claims reference

Check wording against the [regulatory page](../regulatory/trilift.md) first (Aesthetics section). A claim can have a citation and still be outside the quoted indication. Eye Care claims for triLIFT are in `trilift-eye-care.md`. Claims are not borrowed between the two divisions. The IDs use the prefix `TA-` so they do not collide with the Eye Care IDs (`TL-`).

Source materials reviewed:

- **B2B brochure** — "triLIFT 2.0 B2B brochure" (PB-00096753, Rev A per file name; the printed footer shows only the PB number; 2026), 6 pages. A second file ("triLift_6-page_linear_review") repeats the same PB number in a different layout and is not counted as separate evidence.
- **B2C brochures** — PB-00096116 Rev A (face, 2026) and PB-00096124 Rev A (body, 2026), 2 pages each.
- **Clinical paper** — "Muscle stimulation for the face, a paradigm shift in facial rejuvenation" (Mirmanesh), PB-00043560 (printed "Rav A", no year printed), 6 pages.
- **White paper** — "Dynamic Muscle Stimulation (DMSt) for muscle toning and improving facial aging features" (Gershonowitz), PB-00043570 Rev A (no year printed), 5 pages.
- **Older materials checked for the same claims** — B2C PB-00065530 Rev A (2023), B2B PB-00043520 Rev B (2023), eBook PB-00058600 Rev A, pitch deck PB-00053360 Rev A, casebook PB-00043550 Rev C (2022).

"Appears in" lists the newest PB-numbered document that carries the claim first. Page numbers are the page order of the extracted text. All rows are new and `needs review`. The two papers (PB-00043560 and PB-00043570) are Lumenis documents that summarize published studies; every row below was checked against the Lumenis document's description of the study, and the primary publication was not opened.

## Notes and flags

- **TA-N1 (3-5% per decade is not listed as a claim row):** "Muscle mass declines 3-5% every decade after the age of 30" appears in PB-00096116 Rev A p.2, PB-00065530 Rev A p.2, PB-00058600 Rev A p.1 and PB-00053360 Rev A p.5. Its footnote cites "Tufts University, Jean Mayer USDA Human Nutrition Research Center on Aging: 'Nutrition, Exercise, Physiology & Sarcopenia (NEPS)'", a research-center name with no journal citation, DOI or PMID. PB-00096116 Rev A (2026) prints no footnote text for it. It is therefore left out of the table. The Eye Care entry TL-3 and the OptiLIFT entry OL-1 (3-8%) track related, conflicting figures.
- **TA-N2 (claims with no published reference, excluded):** "100% of patients saw visible improvement in glutes & abdominal area" and "up to 68% less cellulite" (PB-00096124 Rev A p.2, "data on file: manuscript under perpetration"; PB-00096753 Rev A reference 5); "94% Improvement in muscle tone & lifting" (PB-00096753 Rev A reference 6, "Arielle Kauvar ... (data on file)"); "X3-stronger muscle activation" (PB-00096753 Rev A reference 7, "X3 than existing system"). None has a DOI, PMID or journal citation.
- **TA-N3 (effect duration):** "The effects of combining all 3 technologies in a single treatment lasts for approximately 3-6 months" (PB-00043560 p.1 and p.4) is the paper authors' conclusion with no separate reference, so it is not a row. The 2026 B2B brochure says results are "visible, natural, long-lasting" with no reference.
- **TA-N4 (reference placeholders):** PB-00096753 Rev A and the white papers cite "Mirmanesh, M. (Year). Muscle stimulation for the face ... PB-00043560 Rav A." The year is missing and the revision is printed as "Rav A". The paper is a Lumenis document and not a journal article. Rows that depend on it also give the primary publication where the paper does.
- **TA-N5 (excluded as out of scope):** the GLP-1 patient statistics, hair-loss and FoLix figures and a per-patient revenue figure in the "Trilift 2.0 and Folix Flyer" (no PB number printed in its text; FoLix is a different product); market-size, revenue and ROI figures in the pitch deck, market-opportunity sheet and B2B brochure; quoted testimonials. The "Dr. James Chelnis" and other case studies carry no referenced statistics.
- **TA-N6 (what was verified):** each claim text, page and reference as printed in the Lumenis documents named above. The primary publications were not opened. No claim has been checked against a published abstract in this pass.
