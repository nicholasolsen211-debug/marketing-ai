# Antares regulatory language and do's and don'ts

Antares is a corneal topographer with tear-film analysis, meibography and pupillography. It is a diagnostic device, not a treatment device. Only a few documents are on file, and no legal do's-and-don'ts list, FDA record or US instructions for use was found. This page records what the on-file sources say and lists what they do not settle.

## Sources

Page references use the page numbering printed in each document ("EN n/24" in the manual; page 1 or 2 of the brochure).

- **User manual:** "CSO Antares Corneal Topographic System, User's Manual / Instructions for Use," English edition, document code 90000011, rev. 003, dated 10/2015 (footer of the last page). No Lumenis PB number and no copyright year appear in the text. Published by the manufacturer CSO S.r.l. (Costruzione Strumenti Oftalmici), Scandicci, Italy. The manual states "The original text of this manual is in Italian" (p.4). File: "90000011_Antares_EN User Manual.pdf", 24 pages.
- **Brochure (current):** "Lumenis Antares brochure", PB-2008216 Rev. B, copyright 2024 ("© 2024 Lumenis Be Inc. All Rights Reserved. PB-2008216 Rev. B"). Three files carry this same control ID and revision: the A4 and Letter print versions (folder "5 Sales") and a 6-page digital version (folder "7 Archive"). They differ in layout only, not in wording of the points quoted here. Brochure page numbers below follow the print version (2 pages).

## Intended use (verbatim)

> The corneal topographer Antares is an electro medical system for the detection, capturing and digital processing of an image of the cornea, for ophthalmologic diagnosis by eye specialists.

Source: user manual 90000011 rev. 003, section 1.2 "Intended use and operating procedures," p.5. The manual does not name a market for this text. It is the only intended-use statement on file. It does not mention dry eye, tear film, meibomian glands or MGD.

The manual's software list under the same section names these modules (p.5-6): Corneal topography; Pupillometry; Videokeratoscopy ("Examination of the tear film with white light," "Examination of the tear film with fluorescein," "Break-up time measurement," "Examination of tear layers," "Examination of rigid LAC clearance with fluorescein"). Meibography and a dry eye report are not described in the manual (see Open questions).

## Classification and regulatory marks (verbatim)

> Device classification in accordance with the rules set out in Annex IX of Directive 93/42/EEC and subsequent amendments: Class Im. The identification number relates to the Notified Body in charge of surveillance (IMQ).

Source: user manual 90000011 rev. 003, section 1.1 "Symbols," p.4. Section 1.2.1 (p.6) repeats "Class Im" for the medical device classification.

> CE marked under IMQ, CE0051

Source: brochure PB-2008216 Rev. B, p.1 (EU/other markets). The manual states that "CSO S.r.l. is liable for the device being in compliance with the Community Directive 93/42/EEC as amended by 2007/47/EC, as well as for the device performance, safety and reliability, and consequently for the CE marking" (section 1.2.4, p.8).

> Rx ONLY

Source: brochure PB-2008216 Rev. B, p.1. Revision A of the same brochure (2017) also carried "Rx ONLY". The text does not say in which market this statement applies.

No source on file states that Antares is FDA-cleared, FDA-approved or FDA-registered, and none gives a 510(k) number or a US regulatory classification. Do not state US regulatory status from these documents.

## Safety warnings and cautions (verbatim)

The manual has no contraindications, adverse events or patient-risk section. Its warnings concern electrical safety and installation. The ones most relevant to anyone describing how the device is used:

> Please carefully read this manual before using the device.

Source: manual section 1, p.4.

> CSO S.r.l. accepts no liability for direct or indirect consequences or for damages to property or harm to persons caused by the improper use of the device or by unsound clinical assumptions based on its use.

Source: manual section 1.2.4 "Warranty," p.8.

> Only units with CSO trademark can be placed and used in the patient's area.

Source: manual section 1.3 "Safety warnings," p.10. The same section requires the computer, printers and other non-electromedical devices to be placed outside the patient area (p.9-10).

> To avoid the risk of eletric shock this device must only be connected to a power supply system with protective earthing.

Source: manual section 4 "Usage," p.16 (spelling as printed).

> Do not use any thinners or solvents.

Source: manual section 3 "Routine maintenance," p.14. The same section says: "To clean the external surfaces simply use a cloth slightly dampened with water."

## Required disclaimers

- "Rx ONLY" appears on both brochure revisions (see above). It is the only prescription-use statement on file.
- The manual states: "The device does not generate or receive electromagnetic interferences when operated near other devices; no preventive or corrective action is necessary." (section 1.3, p.9). This is installation guidance, not marketing copy.
- No other required disclaimer, indication-for-use statement for the US, or legal title card for Antares was found.

## What this means for copy

- **Antares diagnoses and measures; it does not treat.** The intended use is "for ophthalmologic diagnosis by eye specialists." Do not say or imply that Antares treats, improves or relieves dry eye, MGD or any eye condition.
- **Describe what it captures.** The manual supports describing corneal topography, pupillometry, tear-film examination with white light and fluorescein, and break-up time measurement. The brochure adds non-invasive break-up time (NIBUT), tear meniscus height, lipid layer analysis with an optional accessory, meibography with a 5-point severity scale, a dry eye report, contact lens auto-fit and keratoconus screening.
- **Name the user.** The intended use is diagnosis "by eye specialists." Do not describe use by other operators.
- **Lipid layer analysis needs the accessory.** The brochure says "Analysis of tear lipid layer (requires additional accessory)." The light diffuser filter is listed as optional.
- **Regulatory status.** Use only what a source states: "CE marked under IMQ, CE0051" and "Rx ONLY." Do not write "FDA-cleared" or "FDA-approved" for Antares until a source is on file.
- **Product name.** The manual calls the product "Antares" (CSO Antares Corneal Topographic System). The current brochure calls it "Antares +" and the software "Phoenix." Confirm which names are current before using either (see Open questions).
- **Specifications differ between documents.** Quote specifications only from one named document and revision, and see Open questions.

## Do's and don'ts

This table is derived only from the quotes above. It is not a Lumenis legal word list, and none was found.

| Topic | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | A corneal topographer used for ophthalmologic diagnosis by eye specialists | Treats dry eye · Improves dry eye · Relieves symptoms · Heals or corrects any condition |
| Operator | Used by eye specialists | Used by any user or by patients themselves |
| Dry eye wording | Advanced tear-film analysis featuring NIBUT, MGD lipid layer grading, infrared meibography (as the brochure words it) | Diagnoses dry eye on its own · Replaces the clinician's diagnosis |
| Lipid layer | Analysis of tear lipid layer (requires additional accessory) | Lipid layer analysis as a standard feature of every unit |
| Regulatory status | CE marked under IMQ, CE0051 (EU/other, per brochure) · Rx ONLY (per brochure) | FDA-cleared · FDA-approved · Class II or any US classification (no source on file) |
| Classification | Class Im under Directive 93/42/EEC (per manual, EU) | Any classification stated without market and source |
| Measurement quality | Quote the manual's "Class A as per ISO19980:2005 (E)" for accuracy and repeatability error, with the source (manual section 5, p.16) | Unqualified "accurate and reliable" or "low intra-rater variability" with no data on file to support it |
| Patient experience | The brochure's "Offers patient comfort as well as physician ease of use" (marketing wording; no study on file) | Comfort or speed stated as a measured result |
| Business wording | Leave out | "Highly competitive, with great ROI" (brochure wording; a business claim, not a regulatory statement) |

## Open questions for the regulatory owner

1. **US instructions and FDA status.** The only manual on file is the CSO Italian-origin manual (rev. 003, 10/2015), which cites EU Directive 93/42/EEC and gives no FDA information. The brochure says "Rx ONLY" and names Lumenis Be Inc. (Salt Lake City, USA) as distributor. Is there a US manual or instructions for use, a 510(k) or exemption record, and an approved US intended-use statement? Until one is on file, no US regulatory wording can be given.
2. **Is the 2015 manual current for Antares +?** The manual is rev. 003 of October 2015, written for "CSO Antares." The 2024 brochure is for "Antares +." The brochure's specifications differ from the manual's: fluorescein LED 470 nm vs 460 nm; pupillography and meibography LED 940 nm vs 875 nm; weight 6.5 kg vs 6.2 kg; dimensions 515 x 315 x 255 mm vs 505 x 315 x 251 mm; topographic coverage "10mm" vs "0.4 to over 9.6 mm"; power supply "In: 100-240Vac - 50/60Hz - 0.9-05A - Out: 24Vdc - 40W" vs "90-264 V AC ... max 0.9 A, OUTPUT: 24 V DC - 2 A." Working distance (74 mm), 24 rings and 6144 measured points agree. Confirm whether a newer manual for Antares + exists, and which specification set is current.
3. **Intended use vs. dry eye positioning.** The manual's intended use mentions only corneal imaging "for ophthalmologic diagnosis by eye specialists." The brochure headline is "Dry eye disease diagnosis and advanced multi-functional corneal topography" and its dry eye report is described as providing "a comprehensive diagnosis of the dry eye disease." Confirm that the labeled intended use covers dry eye diagnosis, and how the dry eye report may be described.
4. **Manual gaps.** The manual describes the tear film module and break-up time but does not describe meibography, NIBUT, tear meniscus height, lipid layer analysis or the dry eye report, and it refers to a separate Phoenix software manual ("user manual of the Phoenix software code. 90000017," p.15) that is not on file. The "Antares+ Phoenix instructional videos" and "Configuring Dicom on Phoenix" documents carry no control ID, revision or year and are not regulatory sources.
5. **EU directive status.** The manual cites Directive 93/42/EEC (and WEEE/RoHS directives of 2012 and 2011). Confirm whether the CE marking and "Class Im" statement remain current for the device as sold today, or whether newer EU documentation exists.
6. **"Rx ONLY" scope.** Confirm which markets the statement applies to, and whether the manual should state it.
7. **Manufacturer and distributor wording.** The manual names CSO S.r.l. as manufacturer. The brochure names "Distributed By Lumenis Be Inc," Lumenis (Germany) GmbH and CSO. Confirm the wording to use for "manufactured by" and "distributed by."
8. **Product names.** "Antares," "Antares +" and "Antares+" are all used. The brochure's "Antares +" specification heading and the manual's "ANTARES" are not reconciled. Confirm the official name and spelling.
9. **Brochure claims without support on file.** "Accurate, reliable, and low intra-rater variability," "Fast examination process and results," "unique feature called auto-fit" and "fully featured" appear with no study or data in the brochure. The manual supports only "Class A as per ISO19980:2005 (E)" and "ensuring measurement accuracy and repeatability" (p.5). Confirm whether these may be used in copy.
10. **Document control.** The manual has no PB number, copyright year or Lumenis control ID in its text. Confirm its control number in the Lumenis document system, and whether the Rev. A to Rev. B brochure change is the only revision history.
